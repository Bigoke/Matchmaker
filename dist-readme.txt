Matchmaker - portable version (no installation)

1. Unzip this folder anywhere (Desktop, Documents, a USB stick).
2. Double-click Matchmaker.exe. It opens in its own window (needs Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome).
3. Close the window when you are done. Your work is saved automatically on this PC.
   Use "Save project" for a file you can keep or share.

To remove it: delete the folder (and %LocalAppData%\Matchmaker if you also want to erase saved data).

If your antivirus blocks Matchmaker.exe (it is new and unsigned), open Matchmaker.html in your
browser instead - it has everything except reading scanned documents.
